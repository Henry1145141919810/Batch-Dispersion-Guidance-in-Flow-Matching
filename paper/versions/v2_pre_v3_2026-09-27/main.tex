\subsection{Dataset and Preprocessing Details}
QM9 is the DeepChem packaging, pinned by SHA-256 on the archive, both source files
and the built tensor. We keep the 3{,}054 uncharacterized molecules that EDM and
its successors drop, a disclosed deviation. Splits come from one seeded permutation
with $n_{\mathrm{test}}=13{,}083$ and $n_{\mathrm{val}}=17{,}748$, the remainder
split in half. Normalisation constants from \texttt{train\_a}: $\mu$
$2.70376\pm1.53941$\,D, $\alpha$ $75.21779\pm8.20041$\,Bohr$^3$, gap
$0.25126\pm0.04746$\,Ha. The size histograms of the two training halves have total
variation $0.0064$ with mean atom counts $18.003$ and $17.985$, which makes them
interchangeable as atom-count sources. Equivariance is verified numerically: 12 of
12 checks pass to $\sim10^{-17}$ for rotation, translation, permutation and padding,
on both the scalar and velocity heads. Modality 2 uses all 6{,}126 Kenyon-cell
sequences with no train/validation split, because the evaluator is an exact count
and no leakage is possible.

\subsection{Training and Sampling Details}
Guide and evaluator are E(3)-invariant networks of width 128 and 4 layers,
429{,}825 parameters, trained 120 epochs with Adam at $3\times10^{-4}$, cosine
decay, batch 128, MSE on $z$-scored targets, selected on best validation MAE.
Measured validation MAE on 17{,}748 molecules: $f_A$ $0.0897$\,D, $0.2447$,
$0.00390$\,Ha; $f_B$ $0.0840$, $0.2407$, $0.00380$. Both are less accurate than
published predictors because they are undertrained relative to them, and since
$\delta$ scales with the evaluator's MAE our bands are correspondingly wider, which
is a disclosed conservatism. The sampler sweep at fixed checkpoint gives atom
stability $0.9334$, $0.9388$, $0.9399$, $0.9394$ at NFE 100, 250, 500 and 1000, so
quality saturates by 500 steps. Table~\ref{tab:a-train} gives every trained
component and Table~\ref{tab:a-sampler} every sampler setting per arm.

\subsection{Baseline Fidelity and Modality 2 Disclosures}
\textbf{Modality 1 naming.} Our plug-in arm is a DPS-style Gaussian-energy field,
where the original DPS differentiates the residual $L_2$ norm. Our TMPD arm
replaces the constant denominator by $s^2+g^\top\Sigma g$, which is the published
method's scalar affine-property limit, included as a related-work comparison rather
than a faithful nonlinear port. TFG runs at its published QM9 hyperparameters
through our deterministic ODE sampler where the original used a stochastic DDIM
update, and our unscaled one-hot features move roughly 16 times less relative to
coordinates than the released checkpoint's, so its numbers here must never be read
against its published table.

\textbf{Modality 2 deviations.} The standard enhancer protocol is class-conditional
generation at 500 base pairs on 81-class or 47-class datasets of roughly 90{,}000
sequences, scored by Fr\'echet biological distance over 10{,}000 samples per model.
We use GC-band coverage instead of class-conditional distance, 6{,}126 sequences
instead of 83{,}968, and 200 base pairs in the shipped cells. Three further facts
bear on the comparison. Gumbel-Softmax flow matching does not run the enhancer
benchmark at all, reporting promoter, protein and peptide tasks, so only two of the
three closest named methods apply here. Fisher Flow disputes the standard evaluator,
reporting that the cell-type classifiers behind it score 11.5\% and 11.2\% test
accuracy. MOG-DFM's own guided demonstration runs at length 100 while reporting base
distances at 500, so short-length guided evaluation is precedented. At 200 base
pairs the reference classifier global-average-pools and returns meaningless numbers
rather than failing loudly, which is a further reason we do not quote it.

\textbf{Per-seed values.} Table~\ref{tab:a-seeds} reserves the per-seed breakdown
behind every aggregate in the main text. Three seeds is the minimum at which a
standard deviation means anything, and with $n{=}5{,}000$ per cell the standard
error on \ib{} near $p{=}0.1$ is about $0.004$, roughly five times finer than the
one-seed ablation above. Seed-to-seed spread is reported for every arm, including
the arms whose aggregates we do not claim a difference for, so that a reader can
check whether a difference we do claim exceeds it.

\subsection{Algorithms}
\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{One guided sampling step with Batch-Dispersion Guidance. Nothing is trained
at sampling time; the generator $v_\theta$ and guide $f_A$ are frozen.
Modality-specific inputs are the endpoint map and the property $f_A$.}
\label{alg:bdg}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require batch $x_t\in\mathbb{R}^{B\times d}$, time $t$, strength $w$, gain $\eta$,
  setpoint $\tau$, scale $s$, step $h$
\State $v_i \gets v_\theta(x_t^{(i)},t)$ \Comment{one generator pass, shared with the unguided sampler}
\If{$t < t_{\min}$} \Return $x_t + h\,v$ \Comment{guidance window}
\EndIf
\State $m_i \gets x_t^{(i)} + (1-t)\,v_i$ \Comment{endpoint estimate}
\State $F_i \gets f_A(m_i)$; \quad $g_i \gets \nabla_m f_A(m_i)$ \Comment{one guide forward, one backward}
\State $\Fbar \gets \tfrac{1}{B}\sum_i F_i$; \quad
       $\Vb \gets \tfrac{1}{B-1}\sum_i (F_i-\Fbar)^2$ \Comment{the batch is the estimator}
\State $e \gets (\Vb-\tau^2)/\tau^2$; \quad \textbf{if} one-sided \textbf{then} $e \gets \max(e,0)$
\State $\mathrm{num}_i \gets (y-F_i) - \eta\,e\,(F_i-\Fbar)$
       \Comment{$=(y-\Fbar)-\weff(F_i-\Fbar)$, $\weff=1+\eta e$}
\State $G_i \gets (\mathrm{num}_i/s^2)\,J_i^{\!\top} g_i$ \Comment{one vector-Jacobian product}
\State $G_i \gets w\tfrac{1-t}{t}G_i$; \quad
       $G_i \gets G_i\cdot\min(1,\|v_i\|/\|G_i\|)$ \Comment{trust region, shared by all arms}
\State \Return $x_t + h\,(v + G)$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
If $B<2$ the unbiased variance is undefined, and the implementation sets
$\Vb:=\tau^2$ so that $e=0$ and the step degrades to the plug-in rule rather than
emitting a non-finite value.
