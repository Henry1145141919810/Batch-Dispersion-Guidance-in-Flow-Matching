Both are **original to this work**. The rubric requires that any uncited figure be
original, so each carries a source line saying so. Use the PDF where the tool accepts

## `fig1_overview` — the study overview (slide **1f**)

**What it shows.** Two panels.

- **(a) Modality 1.** The source distribution feeds a flow-matching model and a
  diffusion model; a matched protocol selects the family carried forward. Guidance
  differentiates the frozen property net f_A at the endpoint estimate. The plug-in
  baseline applies one gain to (y − F_i); **BDG holds the centring gain at w and servoes
  only w_eff = 1 + η·e**, which is the component the innovation changes. Scoring uses
  the frozen evaluator f_B, trained on the disjoint half.
- **(b) Modality 2.** The controller transfers unchanged. State space, generator,
  property map and decoder are modality specific.

**Colour key, shown in the figure.** Blue = trained by us. Green = trained by us, frozen
at sampling. Tan = external pretrained, frozen. **Red = changed by the innovation.**
That keying is itself a rubric requirement ("label trainable, pretrained, and frozen
components").

> [2] Hoogeboom et al. 2022, [4] Stark et al. 2024.

## `fig2_mechanism` — the controller, measured (slide **2c-iv**)

**What it shows.** Three panels. Panels (b) and (c) are plotted from real measured
cells, so the figure cannot drift from the numbers quoted on the other slides.

- **(a) The sign mechanism.** A batch of predictions around its mean F̄, drawn three
  times: V_b > τ² contracts (arrows inward), V_b = τ² is exactly the plug-in rule (no
  arrows), V_b < τ² expands (arrows outward). Each sample moves along its own gradient
  in proportion to its signed deviation from the batch mean.
- **(b) Setpoint controls achieved spread.** Requested τ_mult against achieved spread
  relative to unguided, for μ, gap and α at q50. **Monotone on 3/3**, and crossing
  *above* the unguided line on all three, which the strength dial does not reach.
- **(c) The deviation gain changes sign.** Measured run-mean w_eff against τ_mult. It
  **passes through zero** and stays inside its bound w_eff ≥ 1 − η. A single strength
  dial cannot do this without reversing the centring term as well.

**Why it matters in the talk.** This is the one picture that carries the whole
innovation argument: the mechanism, that the knob works, and why a strength dial is not
a substitute. Budget about 60 seconds for it.

**Source line for the slide:**
