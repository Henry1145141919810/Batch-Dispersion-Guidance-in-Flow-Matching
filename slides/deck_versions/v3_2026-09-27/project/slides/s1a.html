Part 1 · Set up the study

Frame the study

Problem. Hit a property band around a target, without retraining the generator. A design campaign needs many usable candidates, so the quantity is in-band: the fraction of samples inside the band.

Question. Guidance concentrates a batch; that dispersion is a by-product of its strength. Does making dispersion a requested quantity raise coverage?

Hypothesis. At a centred target, coverage is limited by batch spread, not centring error — so a calibrated spread setpoint should raise it.

Modality 1

QM9 3D coordinates [1]

ℝᴺˣ³ with a relaxed atom-type simplex, N ≤ 29. Invariant to translation, rotation, permutation [2].

Modality 2

DNA enhancers on the simplex [3]

(Δ³)ᴸ, one simplex per position over {A, C, G, T}. No rotation group; every position must stay on the simplex.

Two state spaces, not two datasets: a Euclidean coordinate space with a group acting on it, and a product of probability simplices.
