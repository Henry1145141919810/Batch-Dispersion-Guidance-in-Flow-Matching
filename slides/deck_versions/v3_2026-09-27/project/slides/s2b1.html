Guidance strategy

Rule and target

A frozen f_A is differentiated at the endpoint estimate mᵢ and pulled back to the sampling variable. The target y is the property's q50, one fixed number each. A DPS-style plug-in Gaussian-energy field [12].

Nothing is trained at guidance time

Generator vθ — ours, frozen at sampling.

Guide f_A — ours on train_a; frozen, eval(), gradients off.

Evaluator f_B — train_b; frozen, and never inside the sampler.

Strength, normalisation, clipping

Strength: w·(1 − t)/t [12, 14]. Normalisation: s², the guide's property standard deviation.

Trust region: Gᵢ rescaled so ‖Gᵢ‖ ≤ ‖vᵢ‖ per sample, after w — prior practice [13], shared by every arm.

Window: guidance on for t ≥ 0.5 only.

The trust region is load-bearing: without it, 222 non-finite samples; with it, 0.
