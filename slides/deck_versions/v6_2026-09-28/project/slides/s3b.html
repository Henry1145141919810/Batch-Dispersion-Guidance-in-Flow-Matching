Fair study

All rows share the generator, length, target, band, decoding and reference data. Endpoint uncertainty nearly vanishes late, so plug-in, TMPD and LGD-MC collapse onto one comparator; TFG-MC is a restricted port. Dirichlet FM, Fisher Flow and MOG-DFM are not evaluated here [3,4,5].

Fair discussion

The contracting setpoint (τ_m = 0.5) raises CpG coverage at both strengths and GC at w = 4; τ_m = 1 moves neither. The gains cost 3-mer fidelity (JSD up to 7.73 vs 4.53) and clip hard (CpG w = 4: 7.4% of steps).
