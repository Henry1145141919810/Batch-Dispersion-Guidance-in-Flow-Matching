How we will measure the two models

Both base models are ours, and one held-out evaluator scores everything: f_B, trained on train_b, with the same band and the same metric code for every model and arm.

Control: band coverage (in-band)

Fraction of decoded samples with |f_B(x) − y| ≤ δ. Target y: the property's training median (q50). Band δ = 2 × f_B's calibration MAE, fixed before any run.

Fidelity

Molecule stability (every atom's valence satisfied, EDM's rule [2]) and RDKit validity.

Diversity

Uniqueness among valid molecules, and distinct-valid yield (DV): distinct valid molecules per attempt.

Spread

R = standard deviation of f_B over decoded samples ÷ the unguided value. A diagnostic of what guidance does to the batch, not a diversity measure.

Sample counts. Every cell, guided or not: 3 seeds (20261001–03) × 2,000 samples, drawn as 4 batches of 500, so each arm pools 6,000. The batch of 500 is also BDG's unit (2c).

What in-band cannot tell us. It inherits f_B's error, and a guided arm can raise it by exploiting f_B, which is why f_A and f_B train on disjoint halves. It also counts invalid molecules, so it never appears without stability and validity beside it.
