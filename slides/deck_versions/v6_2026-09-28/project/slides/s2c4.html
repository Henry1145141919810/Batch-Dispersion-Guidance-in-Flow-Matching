BDG at work: the setpoint moves the spread

Our FM, η = 8, w = 1; 3 × 2,000 per point. Dashed line: unguided spread.

Spread follows the setpoint

Raising τ_m from 0.5 to 1.5 moves decoded spread from 0.67–0.76× to 1.15–1.24× unguided, monotonically over four setpoints. EquiFM: 1.18–1.25× at τ_m = 1.5.

Plug-in strength only narrows

Plug-in (η = 0) holds spread at 0.78–0.87× unguided at w = 1 and 0.65–0.79× at w = 4, in all six tasks.

Same calls, exact limit

η = 0 reproduces plug-in bit for bit on our FM; EquiFM agrees within its rerun noise. BDG adds scalar reductions, no network call.

Shown: a wider operating range at fixed nominal strength. Not shown: higher coverage than plug-in (2d, 2e), or that the feedback itself is needed (no open-loop replay was run).
