Motivating the innovation

The limitation

In plug-in guidance [12] one strength w scales both terms, so moving the batch toward y also shrinks its spread.

BDG's idea, and the hypothesis

Keep the centring term; set the deviation coefficient by feedback on the batch variance V_b against a requested τ². Hypothesis: coverage rises at comparable quality.
