What the flow model shows

Plug-in raises coverage on μ (+2.75 points, z 5.22) and gap (+2.35, z 4.04); α (+1.07, z 2.56) is unresolved. The price: molecule stability falls 2.5–6.2 points and validity 1.9–5.7.

Which property decides the base model

Fixed before the diffusion cells land: we lead with the property where plug-in's coverage gain differs most between the two families, with its chemistry cost beside it. Flow matching stays the base model only if it wins there.
