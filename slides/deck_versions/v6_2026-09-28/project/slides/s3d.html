Part 3 · Test transfer and close the story

Limitations and failure modes

1 · The evaluator bounds the claim

In-band inherits f_B's error, and guidance can find its blind spots. Disjoint training halves reduce this; they do not remove it. DNA sidesteps it with exact counts.

2 · Guided ≠ scored

The guide reads a relaxed endpoint (continuous atom types, or DNA off the simplex) while scoring decodes. On GC at w = 1, early guidance added 22.8 points before decoding vs 4.2 in-window: a ~5.4× gap the decoded metric does not keep.

3 · Force, not feedback

Equal nominal w is not equal applied force: BDG's winning DNA cells clip 7.4–9.2% of steps against plug-in's ≤ 0.21%, and apply ~10× the correction share. A cap-limited change is not a controller effect.

4 · The batch is the estimator

F̄ and V_b couple 500 samples, so uncertainty is over batches, not molecules; the registered z is unpaired and ignores this. EquiFM reruns add up to 0.7 points of noise the binomial se misses.

Also open, and reported as such: our VP base model is trained but not yet benchmarked (2a); no useful-yield (in-band and stable, paired) from the current aggregates; and the one-sided, open-loop-replay and signed-strength controls were not run, so feedback is not isolated from added force.
