Conclusion and future directions

Does setting dispersion separately from centring raise band coverage at comparable quality?

No on QM9.
Yes on CpG.

No rung beats plug-in at the registered molecular bar; the contracting setpoint clears it for DNA CpG at both strengths and GC at w = 4.

What the evidence supports

BDG changes one coefficient, the deviation gain, by feedback on the batch variance; algebraically it is plug-in with a batch-dependent gain and target.

The setpoint moves decoded spread past unguided in 6 of 6 tasks, where every tested plug-in strength only narrows it.

The rule transfers across two state spaces at plug-in's network calls.

What would sharpen or withdraw it

Open-loop replay of w_eff(t) matching the closed loop would show a schedule, not feedback; signed-strength plug-in matching BDG at equal force would show reweighting suffices. A matched-force comparison and batch-aware paired intervals would firm up the DNA gains.
