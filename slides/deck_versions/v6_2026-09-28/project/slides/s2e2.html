Fair study, and fair discussion

Reproduced, not quoted

Every 2e row is run by us on one frozen generator, guide, evaluator and target, with 3 × 2,000 samples per cell. No published score is pooled with ours. EquiFM's rows use TFG's predictor pair, so they are never ranked against our FM's.

Three port caveats

Plug-in is a DPS-style Gaussian-energy field; the original differentiates the residual L2 norm [12].

TMPD is the local-linear scalar limit: a related-work comparison, not a faithful nonlinear port [22].

TFG runs at its published QM9 hyperparameters, but through our deterministic ODE sampler, and our unscaled one-hot features move ~16× less relative to coordinates than the released checkpoint's. Never read these against TFG's published table [15].

Better, measured

Cost. Plug-in's calls and time (0.119 s per sample): a fifth of LGD-MC's and TFG's guide passes, about half of TMPD's and LGD-MC's time.

Comparable, measured

Coverage vs plug-in. τ_m = 0.5 is positive on all six tasks but unresolved (+0.45 to +1.52 points, z ≤ 2.44), with 0.5–3.6 points less stability on our FM.

Worse, measured

τ_m = 1 falls below plug-in in 3 of 6 tasks (−1.68 to −2.07 points). TFG gains far more coverage (above plug-in in 5 of 6) but costs 8–27 stability points.
