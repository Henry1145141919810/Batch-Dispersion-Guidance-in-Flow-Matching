The verdict

Same direction, property-dependent size. On QM9 the coverage gain stays unresolved; on DNA the contracting setpoint clears the bar for CpG at both strengths and GC at w = 4. Spread follows the setpoint in both state spaces.

Why GC and CpG: GC is affine in the simplex coordinates, CpG quadratic. The gain is larger on the quadratic property; raw coverage is never compared across modalities, whose bands follow different rules.
