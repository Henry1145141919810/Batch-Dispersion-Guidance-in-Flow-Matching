Why these four

All are recent inference-time guidance rules that steer a frozen generator with a frozen predictor. Running them on our checkpoint isolates the guidance rule, which is what our contribution changes.

Efficiency, measured

BDG costs exactly plug-in's calls: 1,000 generator and 400 guide passes per cell, 0.119 s per sample. LGD-MC and TFG need 5× the guide passes; TMPD and LGD-MC take about twice the time. TFG uses fewer generator passes (800).
