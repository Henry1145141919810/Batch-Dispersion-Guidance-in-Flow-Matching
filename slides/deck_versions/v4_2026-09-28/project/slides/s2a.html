Flow matching goes forward — not for a fidelity win

The row that would settle the family question is still running, and EquiFM cannot stand in for it: external model, twice the data, different probability path.

The reason is structural — flow matching's linear path gives a closed-form endpoint estimate at every t, the object every guidance rule differentiates.

Why we sit below the ceiling

1 · Unscaled one-hot features — the published ablation removing exactly that scaling costs 3 atom and 35 molecule points [2].

2 · Training budget — 303k steps against ~1.56M.

Ruled out: the half-data split (~0.3 points [26]) and our evaluator.
