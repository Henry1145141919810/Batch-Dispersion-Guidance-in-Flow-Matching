Fair study, and fair discussion

Reproduced, not quoted

Every 2e row is reproduced by us on one frozen checkpoint, guide, evaluator, target and sample count. The only quoted numbers in the study are 2a's published base-model rows, labelled and never pooled with ours.

Three port caveats

Plug-in is a DPS-style Gaussian-energy field; the original differentiates the residual L2 norm [12].

TMPD is the local-linear scalar limit: a related-work comparison, not a faithful nonlinear port [22].

TFG runs at its published QM9 hyperparameters, but through our deterministic ODE sampler, and our unscaled one-hot features move ~16× less relative to coordinates than the released checkpoint's. Never read these against TFG's published table [15].

Better

Chemistry retained at matched coverage. A fifth of LGD-MC's and TFG's guide passes.

Comparable

in-band against the plug-in rule — the honest reading of the ablation null.

Worse

TFG reaches far higher in-band (0.535 vs 0.180 on μ) at a chemistry cost we judge unacceptable (0.172 vs 0.348), and uses fewer generator passes.
