Why these four

All are inference-time guidance from the last five years, all steer a frozen generator with a frozen predictor, and all run on our checkpoint — which isolates the guidance rule, the thing our contribution is about.

Efficiency, measured

BDG's two terms share one backward pass: 4,000 guide passes per cell against 20,000 for LGD-MC and TFG. Not the cheapest overall — TFG uses fewer generator passes.
