Representations, conditioning, handling

Representations

M1: coordinates plus a relaxed one-hot over {H, C, N, O, F}. The relaxation is what lets one continuous flow carry geometry and discrete types together.

M2: a point of (Δ³)ᴸ. The simplex is continuous by the assignment's definition, and the native geometry of the sequence literature [4, 5].

Conditioning: none

Both base models are unconditional. The property enters only at sampling time, through guidance.

Deliberate: a conditional model gives the controller nothing to steer, and breaks the M1/M2 parallel we are testing.

Modality-specific handling

M1 invariance: translation by projection onto the zero-centre-of-mass subspace; rotation through squared pairwise distances, with updates along difference vectors; permutation by symmetric pooling. 12/12 numerical gates pass to ~1e-17.

M2 mapping: sequences relaxed onto the simplex, decoded by per-position argmax, scored by an exact GC count.
