How we will measure the two models

Both base models are ours, and every number is scored by our own held-out evaluator f_B — same network, same code, same band for both.

Quality and diversity

Atom and molecule stability, validity, uniqueness, novelty, diversity — EDM conventions [2], bond tables checked entry for entry. This decides which family we carry forward.

Control: in-band

Fraction with |f_B(x) − y| ≤ δ, where δ = 2 × f_B's calibration MAE — pre-registered, so it cannot be tuned afterwards.

Decomposition

RMSE² = bias² + spread², in units of δ, plus spread relative to unguided. Spread is what our innovation targets.
