Not the published benchmark

Standard: class-conditional generation at 500 bp on 81-/47-class sets of ~90,000 sequences, scored by FBD over 10,000 samples. Ours: GC-band coverage, 6,126 sequences, 200 bp. Our base generator is the linear-FM baseline those papers report, but the numbers are not comparable and are never pooled.
