Conclusion and future directions

Does a requested dispersion raise band coverage?

No.

No rung beats plug-in at a corrected significance level. But we can say why.

What the two modalities do support

Servoing the deviation gain gives monotone, two-sided, modality-portable control of batch spread at no extra sampling cost.

It does not remove the coverage-against-fidelity trade-off. It makes that trade-off a quantity the user requests.

Why chemistry survives

The coefficient is affine in Fᵢ, so BDG stays inside the plug-in field's reachable set.

Holding centring at w and servoing only the deviation gain buys contraction from one mode instead of the whole field — which is why molecules survive at matched coverage.

The experiment that could withdraw the claim

The signed-w plug-in, under our pre-registered drop rule: if it reaches BDG's spread at equal chemistry, we drop the controller claim and report the decomposition instead.

Then

Matched clip activity — separate clip-limited from controller-limited rungs. A second seed pair for the open-loop replay. A richer M2 property, nonlinear and motif-driven.
