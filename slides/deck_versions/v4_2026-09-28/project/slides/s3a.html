The transferred method

Unchanged: the whole controller

F̄, V_b, e, the setpoint τ, the gain η, the bound w_eff ≥ 1 − η, the reduction. Zero code change.

It can transfer because BDG's step is a per-sample scalar times the plug-in step: it inherits that step's tangency and needs no covariance matrix and no inverse. That matters here — the natural simplex covariance diag(p) − ppᵀ is singular.
