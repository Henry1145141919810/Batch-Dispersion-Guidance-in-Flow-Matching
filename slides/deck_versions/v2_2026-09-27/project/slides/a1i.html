Hybrid probability path

Native time τ: 1 = noise, 0 = data.

Coordinates: an almost-linear path. The noise z is rotated (Kabsch) and permuted (Hungarian) to align with the molecule before interpolating, an equivariant optimal-transport coupling; the loss adds an angle penalty.

Atom types and charge: a variance-preserving path, β linear from 0.1 to 20.

Sampling: Euler from τ = 1 to 0.

Our local pilot, unguided

256 molecules, 100 Euler steps: atom stability 98.37%, molecule stability 86.72%, validity 94.53%, uniqueness 100%. A usability check, not a benchmark.

Why its chemistry beats our FM

Not isolated by us. It has about 2× our training data, 5.34M vs 3.75M parameters, one-hot scaled by ¼ (our unscaled one-hot is the leading suspect in 2a), and the aligned coordinate path.
