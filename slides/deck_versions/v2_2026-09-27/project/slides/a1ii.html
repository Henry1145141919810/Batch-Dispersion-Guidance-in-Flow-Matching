Running our guidance arms on EquiFM

Why it is in the study. A second, external flow-matching generator asks whether the guidance comparison survives on a model we did not train. It is the v3 backend equifm, running all seven arms [26].

Endpoint estimate m, used by every arm

Exact: algebra from its own regression targets; q_x, q_h are the raw heads.

Posterior covariance Σ, for TMPD and LGD-MC

Exact for atom types; approximate for coordinates, whose aligned noise is not an independent Gaussian channel.

Guided velocity

The model's own velocity map, evaluated at the guided mean m + K·G.

Property pair on this backend

TFG's released guide (tf_predict, time input 0) and oracle (evaluate) [15]. Its band is narrower than ours: δ = 0.157 vs 0.175 (μ), 0.165 vs 0.506 (α), 0.0037 vs 0.0075 (gap). In-band is never compared across backends.

Disclosed limits

Trained on the full 100K, so it saw both halves. Only TMPD and LGD-MC use the Σ surrogate; plug-in, TFG and BDG do not. The charge channel is held fixed during guidance. 1.85× our memory per molecule.
