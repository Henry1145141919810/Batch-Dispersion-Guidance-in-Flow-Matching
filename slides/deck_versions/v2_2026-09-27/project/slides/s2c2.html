BDG: the mechanism

Two batch statistics and a scale-free error (per guided step, batch B)

The rule

A descent direction on the thing that binds. The chain rule carries it to mᵢ along gᵢ; the 2/(B − 1) is absorbed into η, so the gain does not scale with B.

Signs are the whole mechanism

V_b > τ² ⇒ contract

V_b < τ² ⇒ expand

V_b = τ² ⇒ the term vanishes, and BDG is exactly the plug-in rule [12]

Cost: zero extra NFE

Both terms multiply the same gᵢ, so BDG shares the one backward pass the plug-in rule already performs. Cost accounting is byte-identical.

Trainable: nothing

Generator and f_A stay frozen. Two new hyperparameters: the gain η and the setpoint τ = τ_mult · s, a fraction of the guide's own property standard deviation.
