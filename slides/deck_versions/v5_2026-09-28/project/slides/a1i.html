Hybrid probability path

Native time τ: 1 = noise, 0 = data.

Coordinates: almost linear — but the noise is rotated (Kabsch) and permuted (Hungarian) onto the molecule first, an equivariant optimal-transport coupling, plus an angle penalty.

Types and charge: variance-preserving, β from 0.1 to 20. Sampling: Euler, τ = 1 → 0.

Our unguided pilot

256 molecules, 100 Euler steps: atom 98.37%, molecule 86.72%, validity 94.53%, uniqueness 100%. A usability check, not a benchmark.

Why its chemistry beats ours

Four candidates, none isolated by us: ~2× the training data, 5.34M vs 3.75M parameters, one-hot scaled by ¼ (our leading suspect in 2a), and the aligned coordinate path.
