Datasets, splits and leakage

Modality 1 · QM9 [1]

133,885 molecules, explicit hydrogens

MoleculeNet release, pinned by SHA-256. We keep the 3,054 molecules that EDM's preprocessing drops [2].

SplitUsed forn
train_agenerator and guide f_A51,527
train_bevaluator f_B only51,527
valcheckpoint selection; molecule sizes17,748
testheld out13,083

No leakage. f_B, which scores every sample, never saw a molecule that trained the generator or the guide. Normalisation uses train_a alone.

Modality 2 · DeepFlyBrain [4]

Drosophila enhancers, 500 bp

One-hot A, C, G, T; four ambiguous training sequences removed.

SplitUsed forn
traingenerator83,722
valcheckpoint selection10,505
testheld out10,434

No learned evaluator. GC and CpG are counted exactly on the decoded sequence, so there is nothing for guidance to exploit and nothing to leak.

Cell-type labels are not given to the generator. A random split says nothing about unseen sequence families.

Preprocessing. QM9: coordinates centred per molecule (Å), padded to 29 atoms with a mask; properties in D, Bohr³ and Ha. DNA: one-hot on the simplex, decoded by per-position argmax.
