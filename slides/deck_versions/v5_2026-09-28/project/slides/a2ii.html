The honest gap on μ. On molecules neither TFG net trained on: guide 0.0659 D, oracle 0.0728 D, our f_B 0.0840 D. ~20% better, not 2×.

Why theirs are more accurate

Capacity: 2–4× the parameters, 7 layers not 4.

Attention: a learned weight per neighbour; ours sums neighbours equally.

Training: the guide is an EMA at epoch 2,000 [15]; ours stop at 120.

Inputs: the oracle also reads nuclear-charge features.

Why we keep ours on our FM

Ours is disjoint by construction; TFG's only by inference — its saved arguments are overwritten by its own training script, and the oracle's training IDs are unknown. The price is a wider band (ours / TFG δ: 1.12× μ, 3.07× α, 2.00× gap), which is exactly why in-band never crosses backends.

Disjointness test (μ, 3,000 molecules): guide wins by >2× on 30.9%, oracle on 40.9%, neither on 28.2% — against 37.4 / 37.4 / 25.3% predicted for disjoint halves. Replicated on α and gap.
