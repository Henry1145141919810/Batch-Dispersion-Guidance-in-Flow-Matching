Guidance strategy: the plug-in rule

The rule: DPS-style plug-in [12]

mᵢ is the model's one-step estimate of the final sample: x_t + (1 − t)vθ for flow matching, the Tweedie mean for diffusion (1c, 1d). y is the property's training median (q50); s is f_A's training standard deviation.

How it enters each sampler

The same Gᵢ, strength w = 1 [14], cap and window in both: guidance acts only in the second half of sampling (flow t ≥ 0.5, diffusion u ≤ 0.5).

Frozen at sampling

Generator: ours, frozen.

Guide f_A: ours, trained on train_a; differentiated, never updated.

Evaluator f_B: trained on train_b; never inside the sampler.

Why the cap

Early in sampling (1 − t)/t is large, and an uncapped correction can overwhelm the model's own velocity: trajectories diverge and coordinates overflow to NaN. In one diagnostic run, 222 molecules broke without the cap and none with it. The cap is prior practice [13], identical for every arm.
