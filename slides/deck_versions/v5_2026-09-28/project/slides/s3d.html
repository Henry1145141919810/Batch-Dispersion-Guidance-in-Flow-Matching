Part 3 · Test transfer and close the story

Limitations and failure modes

1 · The evaluator bounds the claim

In-band inherits f_B's error, and guidance is an optimiser that can find f_B's blind spots. Disjoint training halves reduce this risk; they do not remove it.

2 · What is guided is not what is scored

The guide reads a relaxed endpoint (continuous atom types, or DNA off the simplex) while scoring uses decoded samples. The gap can hide an effect or fake one.

3 · The cap and the projection edit the correction

A velocity cap or a simplex projection can truncate BDG's term, and a cap-limited change is not a controller effect. Hence clip frequency and realised w_eff are reported with every cell.

4 · The batch is the estimator

F̄ and V_b couple the samples, so results depend on the batch size (500), and uncertainty must be computed over batches, not molecules.

Also open: the base-model choice (our diffusion model is not yet trained); equal nominal w is not equal applied force across arms; the four external methods are adaptations run in our pipeline.
