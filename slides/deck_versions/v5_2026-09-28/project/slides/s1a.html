Part 1 · Set up the study

Frame the study

Problem. Steer a frozen generator so that each sample's property lands in a band y ± δ around a target. The score is band coverage: the fraction of samples whose property falls inside the band.

Question. In gradient guidance, one strength w both moves the batch toward y and shrinks its spread. Does setting the spread separately, by feedback on the batch, raise coverage beyond tuning w?

Hypothesis. Adjusting dispersion separately from centring raises band coverage at comparable sample quality, with the same rule in both state spaces.

Modality 1

QM9 3D molecules [1]

Coordinates in ℝᴺˣ³ plus atom types relaxed onto a simplex, N ≤ 29. Symmetric under translation, rotation and permutation [2].

Modality 2

DeepFlyBrain DNA enhancers [4]

500-bp sequences as points of (Δ³)⁵⁰⁰: one probability simplex over {A, C, G, T} per position. No rotation group; every position must stay on the simplex.

Two state spaces, not two datasets: a Euclidean space acted on by a symmetry group, and a product of probability simplices.
