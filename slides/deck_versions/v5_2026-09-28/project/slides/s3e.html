Part 3 · Test transfer and close the story

Conclusion and future directions

Does setting dispersion separately from centring raise band coverage at comparable quality?

Not yet shown.

The registered runs decide. A null or a trade-off will be reported as one.

What we can already say

BDG changes one coefficient: the deviation gain, set by feedback on the batch variance.

Algebraically it is plug-in with a batch-dependent gain and target.

It expands the batch only when η > 1 and V_b < τ²(1 − 1/η).

It costs plug-in's network calls, in both state spaces.

What would withdraw the claim

Signed-strength plug-in matching BDG at equal chemistry (reweighting suffices), or open-loop replay matching the closed loop (a schedule, not feedback).
