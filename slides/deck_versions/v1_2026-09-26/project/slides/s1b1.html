How leakage is prevented. f_A and f_B are trained on disjoint halves, so the network that scores us never saw the data that trained the network that steers us. Normalisation constants come from train_a alone. The calibration set that fixes δ is disjoint from the sets supplying targets and atom counts.

Modality 2

Kenyon-cell enhancers [3]: 6,126 Drosophila sequences

The evaluator is an exact count, so no train/validation split is needed and no leakage is possible.

We do not merge the two species distributed together. Their GC distributions differ, so steering spread on the mixture would partly steer the species-mixing proportion, which is the quantity under study.
