Residual Calibration for Property Guidance:
A Two-Modality Experimental Study
Independent Research Study
Abstract
Nonlinear properties of denoised means can misestimate quantities needed for
guidance. We test a 28-parameter residual correction on frozen CIFAR-10 and
continuous WikiText-2 flow models. It avoids property Hessians but still differen-
tiates through the generator. We compare property heads, uncertainty, and post-
processing using strength sweeps and three independent sampling seeds. Residual
calibration reduces target error versus a tuned plug-in by 82.5% on clipped images
and 50.6% on decoded text representations. However, a direct head wins on text,
image postprocessing is competitive at half the latency, and fluent controlled text
is not demonstrated. The contribution is a reproducible empirical extension, not a
new general guidance framework.
1 Introduction
Guidance evaluates a desired property on a denoised estimate, even though a nonlinear property
of a conditional mean need not equal its conditional expectation. We investigate whether a small
supervised correction improves practical control while leaving a pretrained generative model fixed.
The intervention learns the residual between a known clean propertyf(X)and its value on the de-
noised estimatef(m). A 28-coefficient head combines compact state statistics with a clean-endpoint
factor. We test the same construction on image variance and adjacent-vector distance in continuous
text, comparing time-only calibration, direct prediction, uncertainty, and elementary postprocessing.
The experiment isolates control, output conversion, general quality, and computation. Originality
priority for the exact combination is not established.
2 Related Work and Scope
DPS uses denoised plug-in measurement guidance [Chung et al., 2023]; LGD analyzes point-
estimate error and uses Monte Carlo approximations [Song et al., 2023]. TMPD derives poste-
rior moments and tractable likelihood approximations [Boys et al., 2024]. Readout Guidance trains
lightweight property heads on frozen diffusion features [Luo et al., 2024]; DEFT learns a small
conditional correction and can avoid generator backpropagation [Denker et al., 2024]. Moment
correction, small heads, and frozen backbones are established.
Our scalar direct head is readout-style, not a reproduction of the published spatial architecture. Our
plug-in transfers the DPS principle to a Gaussian-path flow, not its original sampler. The residual
can compensate for both nonlinear-property bias and denoiser error. Its value must therefore be
demonstrated against simple calibrated predictors, not inferred from a posterior identity or a lower
regression loss.

4.3 Controls, Selection, and Replication
The seven methods test complementary explanations: plug-in tests the need for calibration; time-
only correction tests sample dependence; the direct head tests residual parameterization; added vari-
ance tests uncertainty weighting; and postprocessing tests whether guidance is needed for the chosen
property. All derivative-guided variants share the integration grid, interval, cap, and search budget.
Development uses 160 generated samples per modality, shared noise seed 29001, and six strengths.
Each method selects converted-output MAE; quality and diversity are reported separately. The
frozen comparison then uses seeds 39001–39003, 400 samples per method/seed. Image references
are a fixed 400-image official-test subset, independent of generator samples but not matched to the
property targets. KID therefore measures marginal quality. Small-sample uncertainty and one fixed
generator per modality limit generalization; none of these results measures robustness to retraining.

6 Discussion and Limitations
The useful result and the limiting baselines.The calibration intervention improves target con-
trol over a tuned plug-in, but does not justify a general residual-architecture advantage: the direct
head wins on text. Image postprocessing reaches MAE .0055 at 11.7 ms/sample, versus residual
calibration’s .0049 at 24.7 ms. The latter’s KID change is small relative to sampling variation, and
class accuracy is .527 versus the base’s .547. Text postprocessing leaves all 1,200 decoded token
sequences identical to the base despite near-zero raw-property error. Plug-in guidance lowers PPL
from 9329 to 5236 while reducing distinct-2 from .941 to .865; fixed-index outputs remain inco-
herent. No method demonstrates a universal control–quality–cost improvement. The parameter
budget is small, but inference is not free. Calibration preparation uses frozen-network evaluations of
corrupted training data; the property-only extraction workload is 4,608 training and 2,304 diagnos-
tic model-example evaluations per modality. These counts specify workload, not a newly measured
extraction runtime. Ridge fitting takes approximately 10 ms on cached features; that excludes cache
construction. Guided generation still needs 24 full input-backpropagation operations per sample.
We report these separately rather than assigning an arbitrary forward-equivalent NFE or claiming a
speedup over TMPD or DEFT without implementing them.
The main scientific distinction is between estimatingE[f(X)|x t]and controlling the actual deliv-
ered representation. The learned head is trained on forward-corrupted real examples, while guidance
visits altered trajectories. The direct head may extrapolate differently; the clean-endpoint factor does
not guarantee on-trajectory calibration. Optional time-dependent error variance can suppress useful
guidance and should not be called a calibrated state posterior. Small image KID and kNN metrics
also have substantial sampling uncertainty.
This pilot has one generator training seed, modest generation counts, simple nonsemantic properties,
and an already weak text model. Independent noise seeds quantify sampling variability conditional
on those fixed generators; they do not quantify training-seed robustness. The property-strength
sweeps and CPU pilots are exploratory and excluded from replication claims. Reference datasets
were not treated as newly sealed holdouts; fresh noise measures sampling variability conditional on
fixed generators.
The work supplies a runnable intervention, time-only and uncertainty ablations, a direct-predictor
control, and measured failure cases across two continuous modalities. These controls are not full
reproductions of every cited system. A broader benchmark should implement the closest property-
guidance methods on the same task and evaluate more informative text properties. Larger generators,
more training seeds, and semantic targets would test external validity; their outcomes are unknown.
Conclusion.A tiny residual head is a concrete way to test property-likelihood calibration without
constructing a property Hessian. The experiments should be read as evidence about its measured
trade-offs and limits, not as proof that learning guidance or adding uncertainty is new, that a Gaussian
happroximation is exact, or that denoising improvements imply better generation. The measured
control gains support calibrating the guidance statistic, while the direct-head and postprocessing
controls limit claims about the residual architecture and its practical advantage.

B Development Protocol, Full Tables, and Replication
Development and stopping.Generation validation uses 160 outputs per cell and shared noise seed
29001. Image class(i//2) mod 10balances classes within the alternating property-target groups.
The common six-strength sweep is development, not a preregistered discovery test. Two image
validation jobs supply 18+14 cells; one text job supplies 32 cells. The final 42 cells comprise seven
methods, two modalities, and three new sampling seeds, 400 samples each. No final result is used
to retune a head or strength.
The protocol fixes the image test-reference subset with seed 8129, and uses independently generated
seeds 39001–39003. References are not class/target-matched conditional distributions, so KID is an
unconditional marginal-quality diagnostic. For text, a frozen local GPT-2 scores decoded sequences;

effects, are machine-readable in the accompanying analysis directory. The manuscript uses the full

property is 1.7703 and postprocessing changes it to the target 1.9446, but the decoded 32-position

make nearest-vector decisions invariant to positive rescaling, up to numerical ties; it is a structural
counterexample to evaluating only continuous error.

The independent project contains its own runtime, pretrained model assets, processed datasets, cal-
ibration inputs, and evaluation code.calibration/holds the 28-coefficient heads, property-only
feature arrays, fitting code, and train/validation details.sampling.pyimplements the guidance field
and Heun integration;moment_benchmark.pygenerates and evaluates the seven-method experi-
ment.runtime/contains local model, decoder, dataset, and metric implementations. No external
project directory is needed to run the experiment.
runs/retains raw states, targets, evaluator features, metrics, and model/fit digests. Results were pre-

tored code is validated separately rather than described as the source of a new experiment. The

correction derivatives, mean/variance ablations, norm caps, finite zero-field behavior, partial-batch
counts, paired uncertainty, and decoding. Statistical summaries are generated from saved per-sample
arrays. The verification record also checks that model and head values, sample arrays, and numerical
results were preserved in the standalone package.
