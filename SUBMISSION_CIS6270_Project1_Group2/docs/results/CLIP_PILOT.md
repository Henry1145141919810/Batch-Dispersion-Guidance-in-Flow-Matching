# Does removing the velocity clip help? (pilot)

Generated by `proj1/scripts/clip_pilot_table.py`. Do not hand-edit; re-run the script.

**Exploratory pilot, not a verdict.** n = 256, one seed (20261001), `mu` only, v2's fixed q90 target. The se of an in-band proportion here is 0.0188, so a single cell is resolvable to about 0.0375 and a difference between two independent cells to about 0.0530. Differences at the same strength are PAIRED (same molecules, same noise), so those are judged by the paired z, not by that threshold. It asks whether a full run is worth it.

## 3. The frontier: in-band at equal chemistry

Each entry is the best in-band the arm can reach at **at least** that molecule stability, over the convex hull of its measured cells (mixing two settings achieves the combination). ## 4. What this pilot supports

- **Without the clip the sampler diverges; with it, never here.** Non-finite samples: **222 across 4 of the 12 guided no-clip cells**, against **0** in all 13 clipped cells. `btvg` at w = 16 loses 187 of 256 samples and keeps 0.031 molecule stability. A 222-vs-0 count is not a noise-scale effect at this n.
- **No arm's frontier improves without the clip, under either reading.** Unclipped is higher in **0 of 8** mixture comparisons and **0 of 8** frozen-cell comparisons (section 3). The largest gap, all in the clipped setting's favour, is 0.0487. Against the 0.0530 needed for a difference of two independent cells, **0 of 8 clear it**. What carries the reading is the direction, not any single gap.
- **At fixed strength it is lower in 8 of 12 guided cells**, higher in 1 (section 2). By arm: `btvg` 4 of 4 lower, `lgd_mc` 3 of 4 lower, `plug` 1 of 4 lower. `unguided` is excluded -- it applies no guidance, so the clip cannot act and its tie is structural, not evidence. These are paired, so the test is the paired z: 1 of 12 reach |z| >= 3 -- `lgd_mc` w=4 (-0.0664, z -3.01).
- **Unclipping frees both terms, and the spread term wins.** For `btvg` at w = 4, on the 239 rows finite in both, the residual sd rises 6.49 -> 11.11 delta while the bias moves toward zero (-7.63 -> -7.02). So aiming does improve, but the spread grows faster and in-band still falls. Survivorship works against this reading, so it is conservative.

- **Read `plug` and `btvg` as null, not as small wins.** Their mixture gaps come from mixing: under the frozen-cell reading `plug` is an exact tie at 3 of 3 levels (both settings' best floor-clearing cell is the same w = 0.25 run) and `btvg`'s gaps are one molecule wide (+0.0039, +0.0039). The direction is carried by `lgd_mc` alone.

**Not supported.** Any claim about alpha or gap (not run), about arms not listed, or about an in-band difference between independent cells below ~0.0530. One seed, n = 256, mu only, and the 12 fixed-strength comparisons are 3 correlated strength curves on one molecule set, not 12 independent trials. The mechanism behind the divergence is not established here: the data show that it happens without the clip and not with it, not why. Frontier entries carry no standard error though each blends cells with se ~ 0.0188.
