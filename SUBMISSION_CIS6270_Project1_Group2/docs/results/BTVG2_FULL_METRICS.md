**What `variant - lgd_mc` measures.** The variants share `lgd_mc`'s draws and mean-term arithmetic, so the difference measures **adding the variance term as implemented**, including its effect on the clipped mean step -- not the variance term in isolation. After the first step the trajectories differ; the clip scales the combined field and the variants clip more often; and for `btvg2`/`btvg2_band` the pulled-back variance step also moves the mean. `btvg2_xproj` removes the last of these to first order. This is the wording the repo already uses (status doc, the `btvg2` "What" paragraph).

**`btvg2` and `btvg2_band` have no decoded view.** Their JSONs have no `_dec` fields and their sidecars hold no coordinates; no other copy on disk or in the root tarballs does. They are judged on the continuous metric only, which v2's V4 names as flattering. A decoded view needs a re-run.

## Full metric block

Error columns in units of delta (the band half-width). bias = mean(f_B - y) and residual sd over finite rows; `bias (f_A)` is the same with the guide's own prediction, so a bias that f_A shares is the guide's view, not a guide-evaluator disagreement. On the `dist` target, unguided's bias is the generator's own offset (mu +1.31, alpha -0.02, gap -1.02); guidance changes it.

**Unguided agrees across machines** (0 in-band flips over three properties). **Guided cells do not, already at w = 4:** individual molecules diverge, a few of them chaotically, and on mu and gap the divergence grows with strength (on alpha it stays small). **Aggregates stay within noise:** the largest cross-GPU |z| on in-band or stability is 1.77. So pooling machines is safe for aggregate metrics *of these cells*; a paired, molecule-level comparison of a guided arm should use a comparator from the same GPU. How much that matters, measured: pairing `btvg2_xproj` with the 5080 `lgd_mc` instead of the B200 one moves a paired z by at most 0.44 (mu w = 16, stability: +0.59 -> +0.15), and 0 of 12 tests change which side of 3 sigma they fall on. This is one arm at four strengths, not a general statement about every arm. B200 run-to-run determinism is untested, so on that side hardware and nondeterminism are not separated.

**Reading, computed from the table above.**

- **No comparison reaches |z| >= 3** on in-band (either metric) or stability, in either direction: 0 of 15.
- **In-band leans negative on the continuous metric** (13 of 15 point estimates below `lgd_mc`), **but not on the decoded one** (3 of 6 xproj comparisons above it, largest mu w = 16, +0.0190, z = +1.93). The comparisons share molecules and comparators, so neither count is a test. The decoded metric is the one V4 requires, and it exists only for xproj.
- **MAE falls on mu and alpha in 8 of 10 comparisons and rises on gap in 4 of 5**, none at 3 sigma. On alpha, in the 3 rows where MAE falls by at least 0.1 d (`btvg2` w = 8, `btvg2_xproj` w = 8, `btvg2_xproj` w = 16), the gain is **smaller bias, not tighter spread**: |bias| drops by 0.49-0.85 d while residual sd moves by -0.17 to +0.09 d. The guide's own prediction shows the same (`bias (f_A)`: -1.86, -2.89, -3.36), so this is the guide's view of the molecules, not a guide-evaluator disagreement. The cause is not established here.
