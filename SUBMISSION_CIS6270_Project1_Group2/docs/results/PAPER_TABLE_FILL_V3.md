**Caveats. Each applies wherever its subject appears below.**

1. **q50 only.** No q90 number is used or printed.
2. **Both headline BDG arms, always separately**: `bdg_e4t0.5` (eta 4, tau_mult 0.5) and `bdg_e4t1` (eta 4, tau_mult 1). Never pooled, never only the better one.
3. **w = 1 is not any arm's best strength and equal w is not equal force** (FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md 2.2, 6.2). No table here ranks methods or names a best method. TFG at w = 1 buys its in-band with the largest chemistry loss.
4. **in_band is not comparable across M1 backends** (each has its own property pair, so its own delta), nor between M1 and M2 (M2's delta is a chosen band, `delta_ratio` 0.16). Nothing here is tested or ranked across backends.
5. **EquiFM is not bit-reproducible.** The headline's two BDG arms re-run in the ablation move in-band by up to **7.0e-03** (continuous; 4.5e-03 decoded), the size of EquiFM's BDG effects. Its binomial se understates its noise; every EquiFM verdict carries this. fm is bit-exact (0 difference on every re-run and on eta = 0 vs plug).
6. **`edm` is TFG's released EDMsecond, a BORROWED diffusion checkpoint** (checkpoint `weights/EDMsecond/generative_model_ema.npy`, md5 `6abbd010` as every edm cell records it in `prov.edm_md5`; the local file has the same md5; its arguments come from `args.pickle`, `prov.edm_args`). **Our own** QM9 VP diffusion model is the SEPARATE `vp` backend (md5 `8a3390a6`, selected epoch 1475, 18 cells as of 28 Sep), and it is what fills the "VP, trained here" cells. Both are diffusion; only `vp` is ours. `edm` and `vp` each ran unguided and plug only.
7. **Clip fraction.** M1 `guided_steps` is summed over the n/batch = 4 batches, so the per-sample-step fraction is `clipped_sample_steps / ((guided_steps / (n/batch)) * n)`. `v3_sanity.py` divides by `guided_steps * n` and understates it 4x; not used. M2 counts one `gen_vjp` per guided step per batch, so its fraction is `clipped_sample_steps / ((gen_vjp / n_controllers) * n)`; the script checks `gen_vjp / n_controllers` equals the guided steps of the t grid (50 at t >= 0.5, 100 at t >= 0) in every cell.
8. **Useful yield** (decoded in-band AND stable, per molecule) **is pending**: the per-molecule `*.permol.pt` sidecars stay on blade. It is not approximated by multiplying marginals.
9. **The ablation shows that the deviation weight w_eff, not the global w, carries the effect. It does not show the feedback loop is necessary**: no open-loop replay or fixed-w_eff control was run.
10. **M2 caveats.** (a) v3's window t >= 0.5 against t >= 0: plug gains +22.82 pp from the window, while the best result inside the window is +4.23 pp (`bdg_e8t0.5` at w = 4, vs unguided), so the window is worth about 5.4x the method. (b) plug, tmpd and lgd_mc are numerically one baseline in M2's window (max \|d\| 4.2e-06 over in-band, GC mean, GC sd, k-mer JS, decode confidence and diversity; v_f collapses for t >= 0.5). (c) M2's delta is a chosen band, not commensurable with M1. (d) The w = 16 and w = 64 rows are **one seed**. (e) The DeepFlyBrain-vs-Keras equivalence is in no committed file.
11. **Time** is each cell's wall-clock `seconds` (M1) or `minutes` (M2), per 1,000 samples, **including scoring** (f_A, f_B, second oracle, RDKit). It is not a controlled benchmark: cells ran concurrently on a shared 4-GPU box, and e.g. fm/mu/tmpd's three seed cells took 361/201/201 s per 1,000, and M2's unguided cpg cells, identical samples at w = 1 and w = 4, took 35.7 and 48.7 s per 1,000. Summed, it is cell wall-clock hours, not GPU-hours. M2 cells record **no device**.
12. **The clip is applied after w, so a rung may be clip-limited rather than controller-limited** (FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md 6.2; ABLATION_V3_PROTOCOL.md 1.1 and 4). It is the single most likely explanation for a BDG null and must be offered before "spread control does not help"; the clip cannot be removed (222 non-finite samples without it, CLIP_PILOT.md). The grid's extreme rungs are its most clipped: fm mu at w = 4 clips 23.85 % of guided sample-steps at e8t1.5 and 18.58 % at e8t0.5, against eta = 0's 2.35 % (pooled over each rung's 3 seeds; the largest single cell is 24.03 %, fm/mu/`bdg_e8t1.5`/w = 4, seed 20261002). Every clip fraction below uses caveat 7's denominator and travels beside the in-band it qualifies.
