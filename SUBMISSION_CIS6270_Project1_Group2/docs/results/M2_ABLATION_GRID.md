# M2 ablation: BDG's two knobs at fixed strength

in-band gain over `unguided`, in percentage points, mean over 3 seeds. Generated by `proj1/m2/m2_ablation_grid.py`; n=2000, window t >= 0.5.

`w` scales the whole guidance field; `eta` reweights only the dispersion term. Sweeping `eta` at **fixed `w`** therefore varies the controller while holding the push roughly constant, and `eta = 0` is `plug` exactly. This is what distinguishes BDG's feedback from BDG simply pushing harder -- which matters here because at equal `w` bdg applies ~10x the correction share of the baselines (`M2_SHARE.md`).

## Reading it

- **Down a column** (fixed `eta`, varying `tau`): the setpoint. A `tau_mult` below 1 asks for a tighter spread than the data's own and concentrates samples toward the target; above 1 asks for a wider one and disperses them. The sign change across `tau` is the controller behaving as specified.
- **Across a row** (fixed `tau` and `w`): the feedback gain. A gain that rises with `eta` at fixed `w` cannot be explained by force, because `w` is what sets the force.
- **The `eta = 0` column is `plug`**, so every row starts from the baseline and the whole row is the feedback term's doing.
