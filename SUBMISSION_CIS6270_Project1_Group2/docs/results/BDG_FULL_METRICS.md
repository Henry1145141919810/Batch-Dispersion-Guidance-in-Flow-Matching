**What BDG is.** BDG is plug with the centring gain fixed at w and only the deviation gain w_eff = 1 + eta*e servoed on e = V_b/tau^2 - 1. Here V_b is the batch variance of the guide's prediction F_i = f_A(m_i) at the endpoint estimate, tau = tau_mult x s with s = f_A's y_std, and the per-molecule numerator is w[(y - F_bar) - w_eff (F_i - F_bar)] (review section 4.1). The centring gain on the batch mean is the nominal w at every eta and tau (w = 1 in every bdg cell here); only the deviation gain moves. At eta = 0, w_eff = 1 and BDG is plug at w (gated below). The law's equilibrium is where w_eff = 0, i.e. V* = tau^2 (1 - 1/eta) = 0.75 tau^2 at eta = 4: an asymptote of the law, not a state the loop settles at in the 50-step guided window. The run-mean V_b/tau^2 is 1.27-1.68 at tau_mult 0.5 and 0.54-0.72 at tau_mult 1.5. Per step (the 12 re-run trajectories below, mu and gap only), w_eff = 0 exactly at V*, so every w_eff sign change is a crossing of V*: mu: 6 of 6 cross V* (4-34 times each, at tau_mult 0.5/1/1.5, the first time at guided step 2-5); gap: 2 of 6 cross V* (once each, at tau_mult 1, the first time at guided step 15-16); the other 4 never do: tau_mult 0.5 at both targets stays above it, tau_mult 1.5 at both targets stays below it. It does not settle there. At tau_mult 0.5 V_b/tau^2 comes within 0.038-0.067 of V* and moves away again on every trajectory, ending 1.16-1.58 from it at step 50: mu from 3.34 to 1.91-2.33 after a dip to 0.48-0.51 by step 3-5; gap from 0.81 to 2.04, with w_eff > 0 at every step.

## How to read the blocks

- **Denominators.** In-band (continuous and decoded), mol stability and validity are over all n = 256 rows, counting a non-finite sample as a failure. MAE, bias and every sd are over finite rows only. This run has 0 non-finite samples, so the two conventions coincide here.
- **Units.** Error columns are in units of delta, the band half-width. bias = mean(f - y); residual sd and every sd ratio use the population sd (unbiased=False, the btvg2_table.py convention). sd ratios divide by the same quantity on the same block's unguided cell. sd(f_A) is what the controller acts on; sd(f_B) is whether that survives to the held-out evaluator; sd(f_B_dec) is the decoded view.
- **se.** +- se is the iid binomial se sqrt(p(1-p)/n). Trajectories inside a bdg cell are coupled through F_bar and V_b, so its rows are not independent. Review section 4.9 puts the coupling design effect on in-band at 0.97-1.04, so the iid se is not materially wrong for in-band. On sd the review finds the servo suppresses replicate spread, so iid statements about sd are conservative.
- **Floor.** 0.9 x the SAME block's unguided mol stability, the run's own control (review section 4.6). Margin = stable molecules - 0.9 x unguided's stable molecules. **Knife-edge** = |margin| within one binomial se at the floor, sqrt(n f (1 - f)) molecules. **A row below its own floor carries no in-band verdict.** Sensitivity columns only, never a verdict: 0.3621 = 0.9 x 0.4023, the unguided stability of the 18 `results/sweep` cells at seed 20260921 (NVIDIA B200 MIG 1g.45gb, NVIDIA B200 MIG 2g.45gb, device not recorded, batch 128), and 0.3571 = 0.9 x 0.3968, the pooled v2 unguided (seeds 20261001, 20261002, 20261003, 15000 molecules). Both come from other runs with a different batch, so neither transfers here (review section 4.6).
- **The own floor is itself an estimate.** Unguided mol stability here is 0.4336 (the six unguided cells have identical stability flags and SMILES: the sampler does not see the target, so it is one draw), so the floor carries its own se of 0.0279 (7.1 molecules, against 7.8 for a row sitting at the floor). This run's unguided sits +0.0368 above the pooled v2 unguided, and plug w1 itself is below the own floor in 6 of 6 blocks: read verdicts near the floor as knife-edge.
- **Controller.** V_b/tau^2, e and w_eff are run-means over the 50 guided steps (the cell's `diag`). For the one-sided cell e is recorded after the clamp, so the raw e is shown beside it. disp_rms and dev_rms are the batch RMS of the dispersion and deviation terms. Run-means average over steps whose sign flips; min/max come from the trajectory file where it matches.
- **Paired z** = mean(d) / (sd(d, unbiased=True) / sqrt(n)) over rows finite in both cells; n/a when sd(d) = 0 (the two cells agree on every molecule). Bold = |z| >= 3.
