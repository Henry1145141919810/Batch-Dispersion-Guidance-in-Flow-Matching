**Statistics.** Every M1 verdict is the **pre-registered** unpaired contrast: se = sqrt(p(1-p)/N) per arm, N = 3n = 6000, z = d / sqrt(se_a^2 + se_b^2), Bonferroni 0.05/18 two-sided, **z = 2.99** (FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 6.1). M2 verdicts use the same form at the same bar, **inherited rather than pre-registered** (MODALITY2_V3_PROTOCOL.md computes no verdict; section 4). `above` / `below` = z >= 2.99 / z <= -2.99, else `tie`. Beside every null the tables print the **minimum detectable difference**, MDD = 2.99 x the contrast se (v3_power.py's definition; FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 6.1 and MODALITY2_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 5 require it): a tie is |d| < MDD by construction, so it is a limit on this run's resolution, not a measured equality. Unpaired is conservative (arms share initial noise), so a tie can be a paired win. No paired or pooled statistic carries a verdict here; the Spearman correlations, the property-averaged chemistry means, the seeds-BDG > plug counts and the molecule-stability z are descriptive and **not pre-registered**. M2's protocol states no decision rule, so M2 uses the same bar (section 4).

## Read this first

- **q50 only.** No q90 number appears anywhere on this page.
- **in_band is not comparable across M1 backends** (each has its own property pair, so its own delta and band width) **nor between M1 and M2** (M2's delta is a chosen band). Nothing is tested or ranked across backends or modalities.
- **w = 1 is not any arm's best strength and equal w is not equal force.** Nothing here says which method is best (FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 6.2).
- **Both headline BDG arms are always reported separately** (`bdg_e4t0.5`, `bdg_e4t1`); they are never pooled, and plug (BDG at eta = 0) is the base arm for the dispersion term.
- **The ablation supports "the deviation weight w_eff, not the global w, carries the effect". It does not show that the feedback loop is necessary.** The measured w_eff (seed means, -1.94 to 7.18 over the 12 M1 grids) is not the protocol's pre-run 10^2-10^3, and the high-w_eff rungs may be clip-limited (sections 2-3 caveats).
- **EquiFM is not bit-reproducible**: same-seed re-runs move in-band by up to 7.0e-03, so its binomial se understates its noise. `fm` is bit-exact in this run; `edm`'s same-seed unguided runs differ by up to 1.5e-03 in molecule stability (section 1).
- **Clip fraction** = clipped sample-steps / (guided steps per batch x n). M1's `guided_steps` is summed over the n/batch = 4 batches; `v3_sanity.py` divides by the sum and understates every M1 fraction 4x. M2's denominator is derived from `m2_sweep.run_cell` (section 4).
- **`edm` is TFG's EDMsecond, a BORROWED diffusion checkpoint; `vp` is our OWN trained QM9 VP diffusion (18 cells, 28 Sep). Both are diffusion; only `vp` is ours.**
- **Useful yield** (decoded in-band AND stable, per molecule) **is pending**: the `*.permol.pt` sidecars stay on blade. It is never approximated by multiplying marginals.
- **M2**: on gc, moving plug from t >= 0.5 to t >= 0 is worth +22.82 pp against the +4.23 pp of the best in-window gc setting (`bdg_e8t0.5`, w = 4), about 5x (5.4); no t = 0 or eta-sweep cell exists on cpg, whose largest in-window gain is +18.30 pp (`bdg_e4t0.5`, w = 4), and the ratio is not formed across properties. Plug, tmpd and lgd_mc are one baseline in the window; `tfg_mc` is not full TFG; the w = 16 / 64 rows are one seed; the DeepFlyBrain-vs-Keras equivalence is in no committed file.

Column key (M1): `in_band` = continuous, oracle f_B; `dec` = on decoded molecules; `o2` / `o2 dec` = the second oracle (OC-Flow classifier, its own delta); MAE, bias and sd are divided by delta; `DV` = distinct valid molecules per attempt; `MDD` = minimum detectable difference; `v` = verdict. `diversity` = mean pairwise distance of the centred, unit-normalised embeddings **in the oracle f_B's own network** (`evaluation.embedding_diversity`), so it is a different measurement for each property and each pair: never compare it across properties, backends or modalities. ### Caveats

- in_band here is in `equifm`'s own band (pair `tfg`); it is not comparable with any other backend or with M2.
- w = 1 is not any arm's optimum and equal w is not equal force (FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 2.2); nothing here says which method is best.
- From the headline alone `bdg - plug` cannot separate controlling spread from pushing harder (section 6.2): `bdg_e4t0.5` clips 3.47/5.62/3.55 % of sample-steps against plug's 1.46/0.50/0.75 %.
- **Clip-limited, not only controller-limited.** The clip is applied *after* w, so a rung may be clip-limited rather than controller-limited; FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 6.2 names this the single most likely explanation for a BDG null, so it is the explanation offered for the BDG - plug ties above, not "spread control does not help" -- weighed against each row's clip % BDG / plug (where BDG clips barely more than plug, the clip cannot be what limits it). `bdg_e4t0.5` clips 3.47/5.62/3.55 % (w = 1) and 9.77/12.80/8.42 % (w = 4) of sample-steps on mu/alpha/gap against plug's 1.46/0.50/0.75 and 7.12/4.10/6.43 %; the e8t0.5 rung reaches 16.25 % at w = 4 (alpha); 21 of 153 ablation cells at w = 1 and 75 of 153 at w = 4 clip more than 5 %. ABLATION_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 1.1: compare the clip across the two strengths before attributing any w = 1 -> 4 difference to strength, and read the high-w_eff rungs as possibly clip-limited.
- **The measured w_eff is not the protocol's.** The pre-run figure of w_eff ~ 10^2-10^3 (FULL_RUN_V3_PROTOCOL.md sections 4.1 and 6.2, ABLATION_V3_PROTOCOL.md section 3, BDG_LADDER_MEASURED.md: 1019 at tau_mult 0.5 and 253 at 1.0) is not what v3 measured: this backend's cells record w_eff (seed means of `diag.bdg_w_eff`) from -1.94 to 7.18 over the 16 eta > 0 rungs at both w, and the headline arms measure 1.95/4.67/3.32 (`bdg_e4t0.5`) and 0.04/0.16/0.19 (`bdg_e4t1`) on mu/alpha/gap. Quote the measured values. The protocol's "at w_eff of order 10^3 the clip truncates most of the dispersion term" rests on that magnitude and is withdrawn for this run; the clip caveat above stands on the measured clip fractions, not on it.
- V3_RESULTS.md's "Selection" line uses z = 3.51 (max of 6 arms at the 3-sigma family rate); this page uses the pre-registered per-contrast z = 2.99. Continuous contrasts between the two bars: none.
- **Reproducibility**: same-seed re-runs move in-band by up to 7.0e-03 (0.70 pp) and molecule stability by up to 7.0e-03, on top of seed noise; the eta = 0 control differs from plug by 4.0e-03 in-band. equifm's BDG - plug differences at w = 1 are 0.17 to 1.75 pp in size, so they are the same order as noise the binomial se does not include.
- Useful yield (decoded in-band AND stable, per molecule) is **pending** on blade; it is not the product of the marginals above.

### Caveats

- M2's delta is a **chosen** band (delta_ratio 0.16, floor 4.4 quantum), so M2 in-band is not commensurable with M1's; never pool or rank across modalities.
- v3's window t >= 0.5 vs t = 0, **on gc**: the window is worth about 5x (5.4) the method (plug +22.82 pp from the window against the best in-window gc rung, +4.23 pp at `bdg_e8t0.5` w = 4). cpg has no t = 0 or eta-sweep cell (largest in-window gain +18.30 pp), so no ratio is formed on it or across properties.
- plug, tmpd and lgd_mc are one baseline inside the window (v_f collapses); `tfg_mc` is not full TFG.
- The clip is applied after w: the gc ablation's top rung (e8t0.5) clips 19.21 % of guided sample-steps at w = 4 and `bdg_e4t0.5` clips 9.24 / 7.41 % (gc / cpg) at w = 4, so those rows may be clip-limited rather than controller-limited. Measured w_eff spans -4.08 to 14.24 over the gc grid (seed means); quote the measured values.
- `cpg` has no ablation, so its BDG gains cannot be split into force and feedback. Its largest one (`bdg_e4t0.5`, w = 4, +18.30 pp over unguided) moves bias/d from +0.343 to +0.546 and k-mer JS from 0.00045 to 0.00077, at 7.4 % clipped steps.
- The w = 16 / 64 rows are one seed; the DeepFlyBrain-vs-Keras equivalence is in no committed file; m2_dfb_activity.json and m2_gate_scores.json hold one entry per cell file name, so cells present in both the m2 and m2abl trees collide there and some ablation cells are absent (not used in this page beyond the rows quoted).
- Same-seed re-runs agree on in-band to 0.0e+00 but are not bit-identical (continuous metrics differ by up to 2.2e-04, clipped steps by up to 1).
