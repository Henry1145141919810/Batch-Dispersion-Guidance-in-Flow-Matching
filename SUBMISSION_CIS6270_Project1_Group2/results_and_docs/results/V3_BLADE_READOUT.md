**How to read the pooled t.** It asks whether the difference has a consistent sign across 18 cells that pool three properties, two base models and two bands; it is not the protocol's per-cell test, it was not pre-registered, and it uses the better of the two headline BDG arms. **What the grid does and does not show.** At fixed w, in-band tracks the **measured** deviation gain w_eff: Spearman 0.92 to 0.99 across the 16 BDG rungs, in all 12 grids. w_eff > 1 contracts the batch harder than plug and w_eff < 1 less; where it lands depends on how far the batch spread already sits from the setpoint, so tau_mult does not set its sign alone (at w = 4 plug has already narrowed the batch, and tau_mult 0.75 gives w_eff < 1). The tau_mult 0.5 row rises with eta in **5 of 6 grids at w = 1 and 0 of 6 at w = 4**; the tau_mult 1.5 row ends below eta = 0 in 12 of 12. So the robust half of the README's "sign reversal" is the loss side. What the grid does establish is that the gain is carried by the **deviation term's weight, not by the global strength w**, which the headline cannot separate. It does **not** show that the *feedback* is necessary: a fixed w_eff schedule is not in the grid, and BDG_REVIEW.md found that replaying the loop's own schedule on another seed recovers 69.5-96 % of it. The e8 t0.5 rungs also cost chemistry (column above) and several are clip-heavy (V3_RESULTS_ABL_w*.md).
