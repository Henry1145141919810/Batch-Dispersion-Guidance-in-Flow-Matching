# The paper

The live manuscript is the **submission draft** of 29 September 2026:
[PDF](main.pdf), [editable source](main.tex). Bobo Li, Henry Huang.
The title page carries no version stamp and the figures and manifests are named
without a suffix, because this is the copy that is handed in.
The PDF is 27 pages: main text, references, then the appendix (A.1 to A.9). The
main text runs six pages including floats; the assignment allows five before the
references, so `tools/pagecheck.py` exits 1 on this draft, and Table 5 is the
float that pushes page 6.

This draft puts both DNA properties on t >= 0.3, preserves the original DNA
window in the appendix tables, shows empirical property distributions with q50
and fixed evaluation bands, and includes the completed VP benchmark.

## What this draft reports

- **Base model.** Our flow-matching model beats our matched VP diffusion model on
  molecular stability (39.7% vs 28.8%) and validity (75.6% vs 66.2%), so flow
  matching is carried forward (Table 1).
- **Guidance.** Plug-in guidance raises dipole and gap coverage on both of our
  models, at a cost in stability (Table 2).
- **BDG on molecules.** The contracting setpoint raises mean coverage over
  plug-in by 0.45 to 1.52 points in all six flow/property comparisons, and none
  clears the pre-registered bar (Tables 3, 4, S8). The setpoint moves decoded
  spread in both directions, past unguided at `tau_m = 1.5` (Figure 2a).
- **BDG on DNA.** With guidance from `t >= 0.3` for both properties, BDG is +7.08
  points on GC and −4.88 on CpG (Table 5). The sign follows the setpoint: BDG
  contracts only a batch wider than `tau`, and plug-in already leaves CpG inside
  it. The `tau_m = 0.25` recovery on CpG is exploratory (Table S17). The
  pre-registered `t >= 0.5` window is reported in Tables S18 and S19.

## How it is built

Edit prose in `main.tex`. Tables between `BEGIN DATA` and `END DATA` markers are
generated by `tools/build_results.py` from the result cells; do not edit them by
hand. `python tools/build_results.py --check` is the read-only audit of every
generated block and source hash (23 tables, 1,073 hashes in this draft). It needs the
repository's `results/` tree, which the submission folder does not carry.

`tools/distributions.py` rebuilds Figure S1 from the local QM9 and DeepFlyBrain
data. Figure 1 is drawn in `main.tex`; the other figures are the files in
`figs/`. Files with earlier version suffixes belong to earlier manuscripts and
are not included in the main text.

Build with `pdflatex`, `bibtex` and two more `pdflatex` passes, then run
`tools/check.py` (rubric structure, scope, house style) and `tools/pagecheck.py`
(five-page limit, overfull boxes, dangling references).
