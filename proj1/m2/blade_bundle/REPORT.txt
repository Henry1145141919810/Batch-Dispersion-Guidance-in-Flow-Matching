5. Surprises
   - The bundle crashes at the first checkpoint save as shipped (deviation 3).
   - At low NFE the sampler, not the model, biases composition. At NFE 100, G is
     over-produced (0.240 vs real 0.227) while C is not (0.223), so Watson-Crick
     symmetry breaks and GC mean runs +0.008 high. It converges as NFE grows: at
     NFE 1000, A/C/G/T = .2720/.2267/.2287/.2726 vs real .2725/.2282/.2274/.2719,
     and GC mean is 0.4554. This is Euler discretisation error, not a learned bias.
   - At low NFE, gc_soft and gc_hard also disagree on generated samples: at NFE 100
     the guided property's sd (0.0489) is 6% under the scored one (0.0517). The
     gap closes to 1.5% at NFE 1000. On real data they agree exactly.
     => Calibrate tau and run the sweep at the same NFE, and prefer NFE >= 400 if
        the budget allows. At NFE 100, a soft-scale setpoint will score wider and
        centred higher than intended.
   - The patience-10 protocol would have stopped this run at epoch 195, under
     un-annealed LR (1.92e-3).
   - Val loss moved only 0.06421 -> 0.06337 from epoch 490 to 1500 (1.3%), most of
     it after epoch 750, once LR fell below 1e-3.
